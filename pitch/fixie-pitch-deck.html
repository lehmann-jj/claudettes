The problem with AI today
You can’t type what you can’t name.
Chatbots expect you to type and to describe the problem in technical words. If you don’t know what a “USB-C port” or a “model number” is, that is exactly the hard part.
Why not just a chatbot?

How it works
Camera and voice in, spoken help out
You
Phone camera and microphone
Browser
Speech to text, plus the last 3 camera frames
Server
Keeps the call history and asks Claude
Claude
Sees the video, searches the web, answers
The reply is read aloud by the browser. No typing needed.

Claude is the product
Claude sees, looks up and speaks kindly
Sees
Vision on 3 recent live frames each turn: labels, ports, blinking lights.
Looks up
Web search for the manual once it knows the brand and model.
Speaks kindly
One to three spoken sentences, one step at a time, safety first.
Claude Opus 5.5 at low effort for fast replies. The personality lives in one editable prompt file.
